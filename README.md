# Smart Stick for the Visually Impaired

**3rd-Year Embedded Systems Project**  
*By J Amarathunga – BSc (Hons) in Electronics & IT(Reading), University of Colombo*

---

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Smart Stick for the Visually Impaired** is a mobility aid designed to assist visually impaired individuals in navigating their surroundings safely.  
It combines **obstacle detection** and **environmental hazard detection** to provide real-time alerts, ensuring independence and safety in daily life.

---

## 🔧 Features
- 🟢 **Ultrasonic Sensor** – Detects obstacles within 50 cm  
- 🌧️ **Raindrop Sensor** – Identifies wet or muddy surfaces  
- 🔔 **Dual Feedback System** – Audio (buzzer) + Tactile (vibrator) alerts  
- 🔋 **Efficient Power Management** – Runs 12+ hours on a 9V battery with 7805 voltage regulator  
- ⚡ **Compact & Lightweight** – Designed for portability and ease of use  

---

## ✅ Results
- Reliable **obstacle detection** with minimal delay (50 cm range)  
- Accurate **wet/mud detection** with no false positives  
- Effective **dual alerts** (audio + tactile) – vibration especially useful in noisy environments  
- Continuous operation for **12+ hours** on a single 9V battery  

---

## 💡 Future Enhancements
- 🔄 Multiple ultrasonic sensors for **wider field of view**  
- 🔋 Rechargeable battery system for **sustainability**  
- 📱 Bluetooth integration for **smartphone connectivity**  

---

## ⚙️ Technologies Used
- **Microcontroller:** ATmega328P  
- **Programming:** AVR C (compiled for 16 MHz)  
- **Sensors:** HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor, Raindrop Sensor  
- **Alert System:** Buzzer + Vibrator  
- **Power Management:** 9V battery with 7805 voltage regulator
